# AI-Assisted Research

This project uses AI as a research, drafting, organization, and testing tool. AI-mediated interactions can also provide useful lifecycle observations when they reveal how information, requests, records, or responsibilities move through systems.

## Current case study

- [AI-Assisted Closure Experience — Anonymized Research Note](research/ai-assisted-closure-experience.md)

## Recording rule

AI-assisted experience records should distinguish:

**HUMAN OBSERVATION → AI-ASSISTED INTERPRETATION → REAL-WORLD ACTION → OBSERVED RESPONSE → DESIGN QUESTION**

AI output is not automatically evidence of an external fact. External claims should be verified against appropriate sources.

## Privacy rule

Public research should omit unnecessary personal identifiers, private contact information, account identifiers, case numbers, and details whose primary effect would be to expose an individual. Anonymization is not assumed to guarantee privacy.

## Closure rule

The closure test applies to digital and information relationships as well as physical products:

**USE → TRANSFER/CORRECTION/REVIEW → RESOLUTION → RETENTION/DELETION/ARCHIVE**

A redirect, case number, or status change is not automatically the same thing as closure.
